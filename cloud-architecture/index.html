Cloud architecture

Use the cloud to solve architectural problems, not create new ones.

Cloud platforms offer an enormous number of architectural choices. The challenge is choosing the right balance of scalability, resilience, security, operational effort, cost and development complexity.

Trade-offs

Choosing between valid options

Architecture decisions rarely have one universally correct answer. Lambda or ECS depends on workload characteristics, execution model, scaling requirements, operational model, deployment needs, team capabilities and other constraints.

My approach is to make trade-offs explicit and choose technology based on the characteristics of the system.
