Clear decisions

Complex systems need clear decisions

As systems grow, architectural problems rarely appear in isolation. A legacy platform becomes harder to change. Integrations multiply. Services become increasingly dependent on each other. Cloud decisions introduce new trade-offs.

Sometimes the question is not how to implement the next feature, but whether the underlying architecture is still taking the organization in the right direction.

I help organizations step back, understand the bigger picture and make those decisions with confidence.

Implementation grounded

Architecture grounded in implementation experience

Good architecture needs more than diagrams and principles. Before becoming a Solution Architect, I spent around a decade building and maintaining software systems as a developer and senior developer.

  • Can teams realistically build it?
  • Can it be deployed and operated effectively?
  • How will systems fail?
  • Where will coupling appear over time?

  • Is the added complexity actually justified?
