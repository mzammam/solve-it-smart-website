Independent perspective

See your architecture from a different perspective.

An independent architecture review helps you identify risks, challenge assumptions and prioritize the decisions that matter before committing more time and money.

When a review is useful

Architecture evolves gradually. Individual decisions can make sense when they are made while collectively creating complexity, coupling and constraints that become visible only later.

  • The system has grown significantly in complexity.
  • Development is becoming slower or riskier.
  • Technical debt is influencing product decisions.
  • Scalability or performance is becoming a concern.
  • Teams disagree about an architectural direction.
  • You're introducing microservices, event-driven architecture or a significant cloud decision.
Scope

What I look at

System structure

Responsibilities, boundaries, dependencies and coupling.

Integration architecture

APIs, messaging, events, integration patterns and external dependencies.

Scalability & performance

Potential bottlenecks and architectural constraints.

Resilience

Failure modes, dependencies and availability considerations.

Cloud & infrastructure

Deployment architecture, cloud services and operational trade-offs.

Security architecture

Authentication, authorization, trust boundaries and identity architecture at the appropriate level.

Delivery & operations

Deployability, observability, CI/CD and operational complexity.

Technical debt

Architecture decisions that limit change or create unnecessary cost.
